# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible to apply for remote work. Part-time and contract employees are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by their manager and HR.

## Work Schedule
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they complete 8 hours of work per day.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $500 for approved remote workers. This stipend can be used for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Employees must submit receipts within 60 days of purchase for reimbursement.

## Communication Expectations
Remote employees are expected to respond to messages on the company chat tool within 2 hours during working hours. Video cameras should be turned on for all team meetings unless there is a technical issue.

## Performance Reviews
Remote employees are reviewed using the same criteria as in-office employees. Managers should schedule at least one video check-in per week with each direct report.

## Termination of Remote Arrangement
The company reserves the right to end a remote work arrangement with 30 days notice if performance issues arise or if business needs change. Employees may also request to return to office-based work at any time with 2 weeks notice.
