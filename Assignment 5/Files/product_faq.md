# CloudSync Pro - Frequently Asked Questions

## What is CloudSync Pro?
CloudSync Pro is a file backup and synchronization service that automatically backs up files from your computer to secure cloud storage. It supports Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## How much storage do I get?
The Basic plan includes 100GB of storage for $5/month. The Pro plan includes 1TB of storage for $12/month. The Business plan includes 5TB of storage for $40/month and supports up to 10 team members.

## Can I recover deleted files?
Yes. CloudSync Pro keeps deleted files in a recovery bin for 30 days on the Basic plan, and 90 days on the Pro and Business plans. After that period, files are permanently deleted from our servers.

## Is my data encrypted?
All files are encrypted using AES-256 encryption both during upload (in transit) and while stored on our servers (at rest). Only you hold the decryption key unless you enable account recovery.

## What happens if I cancel my subscription?
If you cancel, your account moves to a read-only state for 30 days, during which you can download your files. After 30 days, all files are permanently deleted.

## Does CloudSync Pro support automatic backups?
Yes, you can configure automatic backup schedules for specific folders. The default schedule backs up changed files every 15 minutes when your device is connected to the internet.

## Can I share files with other people?
Yes. You can generate a shareable link for any file or folder. Links can be set to expire after a certain number of days and can optionally require a password.
