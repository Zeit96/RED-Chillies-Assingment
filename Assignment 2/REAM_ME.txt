In this assignment, I have uploaded two different versions of the advertising image. I felt like both are good enough outputs for this assignment & will be liked by different category of consumers. For both the outputs I have used KREA 2 model. But the raw model was not doing well with handling texts. So for that I used 2 different custom LORAs. Krea2 Enhancer, Krea2 Text Fusion Refusal Reduction, both from CIVITAI. Using these LORAs resulted in good text handling inside the generated outputs. I have also upscaled both the generations using the model I already used in Assignment 1. I have uploaded the KREA 2 workflow for this assignment.
