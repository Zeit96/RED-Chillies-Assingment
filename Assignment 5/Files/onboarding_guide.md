# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day
IT will ship your laptop and equipment to arrive at least 2 days before your start date. You will receive an email with login credentials for your company email account 24 hours before your start date.

## Day One
Your first day begins with a 9:00 AM welcome session with HR, followed by a meeting with your manager to review your 30-60-90 day plan. You will also be assigned a "buddy" from your team who is not your manager, to help answer day-to-day questions.

## Benefits Enrollment
New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health insurance, dental, and vision plans. If you do not enroll within this window, you will need to wait until the next open enrollment period, typically in November.

## Training Requirements
All new employees must complete the following within their first 2 weeks: Security Awareness Training, Code of Conduct Training, and the department-specific onboarding course assigned by their manager.

## Probation Period
New employees are on a 90-day probation period. During this time, managers conduct informal check-ins at the 30-day and 60-day marks, with a formal review at the 90-day mark to confirm continued employment.

## Requesting Time Off
Employees begin accruing paid time off (PTO) from their first day but cannot use accrued PTO until after completing their 90-day probation period, except in cases of approved medical leave.
